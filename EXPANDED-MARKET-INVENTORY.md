## 🎯 Scoring System

**Opportunity Score Formula:**
- 40% Volatility (price movement = trading opportunities)
- 30% Volume (liquidity = safe trades)
- 30% Trend Strength (momentum = profit potential)

**Discovery Threshold:** 0.1 (10%)
- Below 0.1 = Still monitoring
- Above 0.1 = Worth activating

**Auto-Switch Threshold:** 1.5x current score
- If discovered market scores 1.5x better than active → switch
- 1 minute cooldown between switches
